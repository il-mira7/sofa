The Anatomy of a Farfale Sofa

Crafted locally in Georgia. Built on kiln-dried birch timber, German suspension springs, and multi-layered high-resilience memory foam.

🔍 Internal Construction: Frame • Spring Suspension • Orthopedic Foam Layers
01

Kiln-Dried Hardwood Frame (კარკასი)

Selected dry solid timber and 15–18 mm moisture-resistant birch plywood. Zero creaks or warping over time. 5-year warranty.

02

Damped Spring Suspension (ზამბარის სისტემა)

High-tensile carbon steel serpentine springs encased in sound-absorbing polymer clips for optimal weight distribution.

03

Multi-Density Cushioning (შრეები)

High-resilience HR 35/35 foam paired with a cloud-like Memory Foam topper for superior ergonomic support and softness.

04

Premium European Textiles

Bouclé, textured corduroy, chenille, and linen-look weaves rated up to 100,000 Martindale cycles with pet-friendly Easy Clean coating.
